Spotlight · पर प्रकाश

The day the school opened.

14 अप्रैल 2021 — विद्यालय का प्रथम दिवस

On 14 April 2021, forty-seven children walked through a green gate on the main road at Dama, and our school began. There was no inauguration, no ribbon-cutting — only a small photograph of the founder's class standing in front of the assembly ground, taken by a teacher with a borrowed phone.

Five years later we have 480 students, 14 teachers, four buses, and a hostel. The gate is the same one. The photograph is framed in the office.

Academics·शिक्षा

Four stages. One expectation.

चार स्तर। एक अपेक्षा।

Every child moves through four stages. Concept-oriented learning, remedial classes for those who fall behind, syllabus completion guaranteed. We do not promise any "future-ready" or "AI-powered" framework — just basic concepts taught properly.

01Stage 1

Foundational

बुनियादी

Nursery · LKG · UKG

Phonics-first English, hand-grip development, story-based numeracy. No written tests — only continuous observation reports to parents.

Curriculum →
02Stage 2

Primary

प्राथमिक

Class 1 to 5

Concept-oriented learning in English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS. Basic computer familiarity from Class 3. Remedial support is built into the timetable.

Curriculum →
03Stage 3

Middle

माध्यमिक

Class 6 to 8

Subject teachers for Science, Social Science, Mathematics, English, Hindi, Sanskrit. Remedial classes held twice a week for students who fall behind.

Curriculum →
04Stage 4

Secondary

उच्च प्राथमिक

Class 9 to 10

Board-oriented preparation following CBSE / JAC patterns. Guaranteed timely syllabus completion. Special doubt-clearing classes held on Saturdays.

Scholarships & fee relief

No child turned away for fee.

शुल्क के कारण किसी बच्चे को वंचित नहीं किया जाएगा।

Four categories of students receive additional relief, and one category receives 100% free education.

See fee structure →
  • 01

    Free Education for Orphan Children

    अनाथ बच्चों के लिए निःशुल्क शिक्षा

    Tuition, books, uniform, and bus fee fully waived. Apply with certificate from Block Development Officer.

  • 02

    Merit Scholarship, Class 10

    मेरिट छात्रवृत्ति, कक्षा 10

    Awarded to top three students of Class 9 based on annual exam rank. Covers tuition for Class 10.

  • 03

    Sibling Discount

    बहन-भाई छूट

    10% tuition concession for the second child enrolled from the same family.

  • 04

    Single-Parent Concession

    एकल-अभिभावक छूट

    Half tuition concession with valid documentation. Subject to Principal approval.
